## PART 2 — THE 7 WEAKNESSES

### W1 — Too Many Features, No Clear Product

**Symptom:** 16 AI Products + 12 AI Tools + 138 exams + 8 formats + 34 languages. Nobody knows what Charvak is for.

**Root cause:** Every session shipped something, but no session shipped positioning.

**Strength conversion:** "Charvak is the placement operating system for Indian colleges."

This is the positioning. Every feature either supports placement or becomes hidden.

**Concrete action:**
1. Rewrite homepage hero: "The Placement Operating System for Indian Colleges"
2. 3 pillars become: Assess to Prove to Place (drop For Learners as separate pillar)
3. Move 16 AI products + 12 AI tools into a More from Charvak section
4. Every future session starts with: How does this support placement?

**Effort:** 1 session. **Trigger:** NOW.

---

### W2 — No Product-Led Growth Motion

**Symptom:** 674 monthly users, no viral loop, no free-to-paid conversion, no daily habit.

**Root cause:** Every feature exists standalone. Nothing connects acquisition to retention to revenue.

**Strength conversion:** The Free to Assess to Share to Enroll loop.

Free Career Assessment
  --> See result
  --> Share to unlock full report
  --> Friend lands on assessment
  --> Takes free assessment
  --> Cert shared to LinkedIn
  --> University/employer sees Charvak
  --> College signs up
  --> Students use free tier
  --> Loop repeats

**Concrete action:**
1. Career Assessment: add unlock-full-report-by-sharing gate
2. Cert: add share-to-LinkedIn primary CTA
3. Free daily drill: persistent habit loop with streak + leaderboard
4. Referral: Rs 100 credit per signup
5. Add PWA for mobile retention

**Effort:** 4-5 sessions. **Trigger:** NOW.

---

### W3 — 1-Person Team

**Symptom:** Every feature built by 1 person. No sales, no marketing, no CS. Cannot scale beyond 3-5 customers.

**Root cause:** Business model has not yet generated revenue to hire.

**Strength conversion:** Do not hire. Compound.

**Plan:**
1. First hire: Sales (contract or part-time) — 20% commission on signed deals. No salary, only commission. Pays for itself.
2. Second hire: Content writer — Rs 15-25K/mo, produces 8 blog posts + 1 case study per month.
3. Third hire: CSM — after 5+ paying customers, full-time.

**Concrete action:**
1. Find 1 commission-only salesperson on LinkedIn
2. Find 1 freelance content writer on Upwork
3. Do NOT hire engineer until 10+ paying customers

**Effort:** 1 week of search + onboarding. **Trigger:** After first paying customer.

---

### W4 — No Brand Identity

**Symptom:** Generic logo, generic homepage, no design system, no messaging that sticks.

**Strength conversion:** Build the Charvak Identity System in 3 sessions.

**Session 1 — Logo + colors:** Commission Fiverr designer (Rs 5-10K). Lock in Charvak Teal (#3ba591), Dark (#1a1a2e), Amber (#f59e0b). Font: Poppins + Inter.

**Session 2 — Design system:** Create static/css/charvak-design-system.css with 20 classes. Every page uses these.

**Session 3 — Messaging:** 1 tagline + 1 elevator pitch + 1 manifesto (500 words).

**Effort:** 3 sessions + Rs 5-10K design. **Trigger:** After 1 paying customer.

### W5 — Zero Case Studies

**Symptom:** Even Amrita has not signed. Every sales call is imagine what this could do.

**Root cause:** Chicken-and-egg. Need customer to get case study, need case study to get customer.

**Strength conversion:** Build Design Partner Program.

**Offer:** 3 universities get free full-access pilot for 8 weeks. In exchange: 1 case study + 1 video testimonial + 3 referrals. Value: Rs 15L worth of Charvak for free, in exchange for a written case study.

**Concrete action:**
1. Target 3 colleges: Amrita + IIT Madras CDC + VIT Vellore CDC
2. Send Design Partner email to each
3. Onboarding: 100-student cohort, 8-week pilot
4. Case study: X percent improvement in placement readiness in 8 weeks
5. Publish to /case-studies page (build in 1 session)

**Effort:** 1 session + 3 email sends. **Trigger:** NOW.

### W7 — No Data Moat

**Symptom:** 21 public passports (mostly seeded). 3 demo institutions. No real customer data means no network effect.

**Root cause:** No real customers = no real data.

**Strength conversion:** Turn 21 passports into 10,000 verified candidate profiles in 12 months.

**The compounding:**
- 674 users today
- +50 percent monthly growth (via viral loop after W2 fix) = 674 x 1.5^12 = 87,000 users in 12 months
- At 30 percent passport creation = 26,000 passports
- At 10 percent employer view rate = 2,600 real employer verifications

At 10,000+ passports: employer network has real moat, universities see it as a talent pool, investors see data network effect.

**Concrete action:**
1. Fix W2 to unlock growth
2. Make passport creation automatic on cert generation
3. Track every certificate view
4. Every month, publish Charvak Data Report

**Effort:** Nothing new to build — compound existing data. **Trigger:** After W2 fix.

## PART 5 — THE ONE-PARAGRAPH STRATEGY

Charvak's single biggest gap is not a feature — it is revenue proof. Every
weakness is a symptom of great-product-no-business. The conversion
sequence reverses this: close 1 paying customer (Week 1), build recurring
infrastructure (Week 2-4), compound data via viral loop (Month 2-3), add
sales and case studies (Month 3), hit Rs 5-15L ARR (Month 3).

The rest of the roadmap (mobile app, SOC 2, Voice AI, LMS bridge) waits
for revenue. Nothing else matters until 1 paying customer exists.

Every session should ask: Does this help close Amrita / next 3
universities? If yes, build. If no, defer.
