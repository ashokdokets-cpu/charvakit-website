## Full-Time Hire (FTH) - Tiered by level

| Level | Years | Fee (% CTC) | Minimum | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fresher / Intern | 0-1 | 6.5% | Rs 10,000 | Bulk hires |
| Junior | 1-3 | 8.0% | Rs 25,000 | Standard |
| Mid | 3-6 | 8.33% | Rs 40,000 | Standard |
| Senior | 6-10 | 9.5% | Rs 75,000 | + Reference check |
| Lead / Principal | 10+ | 12.0% | Rs 1,50,000 | + Interview coordination |
| Architect / Staff | 12+ | 15.0% | Rs 2,50,000 | + Extended panel support |
| Manager / Director | 15+ | 18.0% | Rs 4,00,000 | Bespoke search |

**Standard terms:**
- 90-day replacement guarantee (Senior+: 90 days; Manager+: 60 days)
- Payment: 30 days from candidate joining

**Volume discounts (per year, per client):**
- 5+ hires: 5% off total
- 10+ hires: 10% off total
- 15+ hires (MSP arrangement): 15% off + priority SLA

---

## Contract-to-Hire (CTH)

**Monthly contract rate:** 1.4x full-time equivalent monthly cost
**Conversion fee:** FTH rate at conversion, prorated by months worked
**Rolloff:** no penalty if candidate doesn't work out

## Contract (C2C / W2)
- Charvak margin: 20-25% over vendor cost
- Monthly invoiced: No placement fee

---

## Rush / Priority Options
- 24-hour SLA (any level): +30% fee
- Weekend shortlist: +20% fee
- Dedicated MSP arrangement: 15% off + priority SLA

---

## Volume arrangement (MSP)
**Criteria:** 15+ hires per year, 12-month commitment
- 15% discount across all engagement models
- Priority SLA, dedicated account manager, quarterly review

---

## Payment Terms
- FTH: 30 days from joining
- CTH: Monthly (net 15)
- Micro-internship: 100% upfront in escrow
- Contract: Monthly (net 30)

**Payment methods:** Razorpay, PayPal, NEFT/RTGS, Wire transfer

---

## What to say when a client asks about rates
Our fees depend on the role level and engagement model. For a first engagement, we usually recommend a 2-4 week micro-internship trial so you see the candidate's work before committing to full-time.
