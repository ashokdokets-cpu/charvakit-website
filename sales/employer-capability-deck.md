## 3. The 3-Stage Vetting Stack

Every candidate from Charvak passes through 3 stages before
you see them. This is how we reduce your screening time by 80%.

### Stage 1 — JD-to-Candidate Match (AI)
- Paste the JD (or we scrape from your ATS)
- AI parses skills, seniority, domain
- Candidates auto-scored against the JD
- Top 20% advance to stage 2

### Stage 2 — Role-Calibrated Assessment
- Take a live assessment calibrated to the role
- Judge0 code test for technical roles (real execution)
- SJT + behavioral + integrity signals
- Deterministic scoring — no reviewer bias
- Generates a verifiable certificate (HMAC-signed)

### Stage 3 — Human Review + Client-Ready Shortlist
- Charvak team reviews top candidates
- Optional reference check + work sample review
- You get a shortlist of 3-5 candidates with:
  - Resume
  - Verifiable certificate
  - Code submission (Judge0 sandbox)
  - Integrity summary
  - Hiring recommendation
