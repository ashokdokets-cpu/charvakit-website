About Charvak

Charvak IT Consulting is a full-service web designing and IT staffing company based in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh, India. We are experienced and forward-thinking technologists and talent acquisition professionals with a deep understanding of our client's needs and developing effective solutions. We are a team of award-winning web designers, app developers, and staffing experts, and we know what it takes to transform your ideas into real-time results.

Through our years of experience, we've also learned every business and client has unique challenges to overcome and benchmarks to achieve. Be it a custom application development or finding the right fit, at Charvak, we strategize every phase to deliver the best than what we promise. We've established a transparent and unified channel to break down communication barriers.

Both your website and team are "the most" vital factors to persuade users to purchase your products or call for your service. At Charvak, we build web portals and mobile apps that envision your growth; and source the best talent in the market to build an industry-leading team. We aimed to serve small and mid-size companies with limited capabilities and position them as market leaders. We go the extra mile and blend the right set of tools and people to develop customer-centric and cost-effective solutions. Charvak supports businesses from ideation to designing mock-ups to development to deployment and maintenance.
