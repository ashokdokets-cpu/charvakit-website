## WHERE ZERAVIA IS WEAK (Charvak's Moat)

1. **No HMAC signing** — certificates are PDFs with third-party logos
2. **No assessment engine** — Judge0 is code execution, not skill assessment
3. **No JD calibration** — Intelligence Engine is search, not fit-check
4. **No abuse protection** — no attempt limits, identity binding, integrity signals
5. **No employer dashboard** — 180 hiring partners claim, no visible tooling
6. **Job board is a wrapper** — "Apply via Adzuna" means they don't own it
7. **Claims are suspicious** — Rs 8.4 LPA avg CTC at 1-2 years old is very high
8. **Testimonials are one-liners** — no LinkedIn links, no verifiable evidence
9. **Cert claims ambiguous** — "Microsoft certified" likely means prep, not issued cert
10. **No public verify URL** — no equivalent of /readiness/{cert_id}

**Trigger:** Before next B2C email batch
**ROI:** Converts curiosity into subscriptions
