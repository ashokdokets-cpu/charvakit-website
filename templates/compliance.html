Compliance standards

EU AI Act

Aligned to transparency, human oversight, and risk classification requirements for AI hiring systems.

  • Transparency: every score traceable to evidence
  • Human oversight: decisions remain yours
  • Bias monitoring: statistical fairness checks
  • Data governance: retention policies enforced

NYC Local Law 144

Aligned to NYC automated employment decision tool requirements for bias audits and candidate notice.

  • Bias audit available on request
  • Adverse impact ratio published per year
  • Candidate notice provided at assessment start
  • Data retention within NYC-mandated limits

India DPDP Act

Aligned to India Digital Personal Data Protection Act for consent, purpose limitation, and data principal rights.

  • Explicit consent captured before assessment
  • Purpose limitation: data used only for hiring
  • Right to access, correct, and erase
  • Data stored in India-region database

GDPR

Aligned to EU General Data Protection Regulation for data subject rights, transfers, and transparency.

  • Right to access, erasure, portability
  • Data processing agreements available
  • EU-region data residency on request
  • 72-hour breach notification

Available on request. India-region by default; EU and US regions available for enterprise contracts.

  • US region: available on enterprise contract

Candidate rights

Candidates have full control over their assessment data. Rights are enforced, not just promised.

  • Access: download all assessment data
  • Erasure: delete account and all data
  • Portability: export in machine-readable format
  • Correction: fix any inaccurate data
