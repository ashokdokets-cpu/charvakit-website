What is a Role Readiness Score?

A Role Readiness Score benchmarks your current skills against real market data for a specific role x industry x level combination. Unlike a raw MCQ score, it accounts for topic coverage, AI-scored depth, and your learning trajectory over time.

The output is a public, verifiable certificate with an HMAC-signed hash. Any employer can check its authenticity with a single URL. No login required to view, no paywall to share.

Common questions

Is this really free?
Yes. The 5-minute check and the resulting certificate are completely free. No credit card, no account upgrade. Premium reports and matched jobs are paid add-ons, but the readiness certificate itself is free forever.
How long does it take?
About 5 minutes end to end. The assessment is 10 questions. The certificate generates instantly after you answer.
Who can see my certificate?
Only people you share the URL with. The certificate page shows your first name and last initial, your target role, and your score. It never exposes your email or full name.
Can I retake the check?
Yes, anytime. Each retake produces a new certificate, and your score history accumulates. Improvement over time is a good signal employers like to see.
