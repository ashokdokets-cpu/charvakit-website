Accessibility Statement

WCAG 2.1 AA Compliant

Our Commitment

Charvak IT Consulting Pvt Ltd is committed to digital accessibility for all users, including those with disabilities. We follow WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards.

Accessibility Features

  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Screen reader compatibility
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios
  • Resizable text without loss of functionality
  • Alt text for all images
  • ARIA labels on interactive elements
  • RTL support for Arabic and Hebrew
  • 34 language translations

Feedback

Report accessibility issues: hr@charvakit.com

We aim to respond within 2 business days.
