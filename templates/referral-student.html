Student FAQ

When do credits land?

Signup credits: instant. Cert credits: within 24 hours of payment confirmation. Milestones: within 24 hours of trigger. All credits appear in your credit dashboard.

What can I use credits for?

Any paid AI tool (career assessments, IELTS deep reports, resume roast), mock drives, AI courses, and readiness certificates. 100 credits ≈ ₹50 in value.

What counts as a "paid cert"?

Any certificate from Charvak that costs credits: career certifications (5 credits for 70-84 score tier), readiness checks (25 credits), IELTS deep reports (99 credits at 400 credits value), or FYP certifications.

How does depth-2 work?

If your friend (depth 1) refers someone else (depth 2) and that person signs up, you earn 25 credits. It doesn't go deeper than 2 levels.

Can I combine credits + commission?

Yes. As a student you earn credits. If you also refer institutions or employers, you earn commission separately. Both stack — see the dashboard for both.
