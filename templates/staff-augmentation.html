Direct Hire

If you have multiple projects to develop and need a resource on a long-term basis, direct hiring is ideal for you. In this staffing model, the candidate goes directly on the client's payroll.

Contract or Temporary Hiring

Best staff augmentation method where companies look for external tech resources on a demand basis for a short period. Flexible method that saves time and cost.

Contract-to-Hire

"Try before you buy" model. Hire talent on contract basis, check their proficiency, and proceed with permanent hire if satisfied. Saves time and cost in training and onboarding.

Hybrid Staffing Model

Combination of direct and contract staffing models. Companies can prioritize and plan their hiring process and secure roles for future projects.
