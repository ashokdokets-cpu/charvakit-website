Frequently Asked Questions

What if the hire doesn't work out?

90-day replacement guarantee. If the candidate leaves or is terminated within 90 days of joining, we replace them at no cost or refund 100% of the fee.

How do you verify skills?

Every candidate takes an AI assessment calibrated to their target role — 106 roles × 66 industries × 8 formats. Results are cryptographically signed (HMAC). Any employer can verify the certificate at charvakit.com/passport/{handle}. We do not accept self-reported skills.

How much does it cost?

8% of first-year CTC, invoiced after you hire. Net-30 payment terms. No other fees — no subscriptions, no retainers, no posting fees.

Can I try before hiring?

Yes. Most roles can be structured as a 2-week micro-internship with escrow. You pay only after successful delivery. If you then hire full-time, the micro-internship fee is credited toward the 8% placement fee.

How are candidates sourced?

Three channels: (1) verified students from our partner colleges (Amrita, VIT, NIT Trichy, Eswar), (2) our B2C funnel (find-jobs → verified-jobs), and (3) legal job APIs (Naukri/NCS). Every candidate is pre-screened and has a verifiable certificate.
