Frequently asked

How do I get my referral link?

Sign in and visit /referral-dashboard. Your unique code (e.g., CHV-A1B2C3D4) is generated automatically. Add ?ref=YOUR_CODE to any Charvak URL to start tracking.

How long does attribution last?

90 days from first click (first-touch attribution). If someone you referred visits Charvak and converts within 90 days of their first click on your link, you earn commission.

When do I get paid?

Credits (student referrals): instant to your credit balance. Commission (institution/employer): 30-day fraud hold, then approved. Payouts: minimum ₹1,000, processed weekly via UPI/bank/PayPal.

What's the anti-fraud policy?

Self-referrals are blocked (email + IP match). Volume anomalies trigger manual review. Refunded deals claw back commission. Accounts can be suspended or banned for abuse. All legitimate referrals are fully protected.

Is this open globally?

Yes. Payouts via Razorpay (INR) or PayPal (USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SGD, AUD, JPY). No geographic restrictions. Tier thresholds scale by country.

Can I be in multiple programs?

Yes. A student referrer can also refer institutions and become an affiliate. Each program has its own dashboard view — all under the same account.
