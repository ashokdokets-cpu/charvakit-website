Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information
  • Name and contact details
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business information
  • Payment information (processed securely via Razorpay)
1.2 Automatically Collected Information
  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device information
  • Usage patterns and preferences
  • Cookies and similar technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our services
  • To process escrow transactions securely
  • To communicate with you about your transactions
  • To improve our AI-powered dispute resolution
  • To send important updates and notifications
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To prevent fraud and ensure security

3. Payment Information

Important: We do not store your complete payment card details. All payment processing is handled by Razorpay, our authorized payment gateway partner. Razorpay is PCI-DSS compliant and follows industry-standard security practices.

For escrow transactions through Dokets VouchAI:

  • Payment details are encrypted and processed by Razorpay
  • We only receive transaction confirmation and status
  • Bank account details for fund disbursement are stored securely

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure servers with access controls
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • AI-powered fraud detection systems

5. Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay for transaction processing

  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect rights
  • Transaction Parties: Relevant buyer/seller information for escrow transactions

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Cookies Policy

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure security of transactions

You can control cookies through your browser settings.

GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

Under GDPR, EU residents have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion ("Right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive data in machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing for marketing
  • Restriction: Limit processing in certain circumstances

To exercise GDPR rights, email hr@charvakit.com with subject "GDPR Request".

Under CCPA, California residents have the right to:

Charvak does NOT sell personal information. To exercise CCPA rights, email hr@charvakit.com.
